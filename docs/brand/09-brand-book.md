# 09. Brand Book

## Purpose

Provide the official consolidated brand guide for the Diabetes Universe ecosystem.

This Brand Book summarizes approved brand architecture, logo architecture, brand
identity, and brand governance without creating final graphic assets, selecting
colors or typefaces, or introducing new architectural decisions.

Authoritative requirements remain in documents `05`–`08`. This document links to
them and consolidates approved brand rules for practical reference.

## Status

Feature Complete

## Brand Overview

The Brand Book is the official guide for applying the **Diabetes Universe** brand
across products, services, documentation, marketing, and partner materials.

It exists to:

- give teams one consolidated reference for approved brand rules;
- align communication, visual philosophy, and usage principles with upstream
  specifications;
- prevent duplication of normative content from documents `05`–`08`;
- support consistent brand application without producing final assets in this
  lifecycle stage.

The official brand name is **Diabetes Universe**. Rebranding and alternative names
are not under consideration.

## Brand Strategy Summary

From [05 Brand Architecture Specification](05-brand-architecture-specification.md):

- **Strategic objective** — create a recognizable international digital ecosystem
  for people with diabetes that can exist for decades and scale across all
  approved product surfaces.
- **Immutable decisions** — the brand is international, not bound to one country,
  language, diabetes type, or age group, and designed for long-term development.
- **Differentiation** — a unified long-term ecosystem connecting data, daily
  actions, education, services, and assistance without overwhelming the user.
- **Scaling** — one master brand across web, mobile, desktop, AI, marketplace,
  education, medical services, API, documentation, marketing, and future products.
- **Trust and safety** — information must be separated from medical
  recommendation; AI limitations must not be hidden; users must be directed to
  physicians where professional judgment is required.

This summary does not extend or alter document `05`.

## Brand DNA

| Element                  | Approved definition                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Mission**              | Make life with diabetes simpler, safer, and calmer.                                                                                              |
| **Vision**               | Create the most reliable, convenient, and recognizable digital ecosystem for people with diabetes in the world.                                  |
| **Values**               | Trust, accuracy, simplicity, modern technology, reliability, care                                                                                |
| **Promise**              | Every interaction helps the user better understand their situation, reduce uncertainty, and feel more in control without false medical promises. |
| **Primary archetype**    | **Sage** — clarity, knowledge, and accuracy                                                                                                      |
| **Supporting archetype** | **Caregiver** — support, safety, and care                                                                                                        |
| **Emotional territory**  | Calm, confidence, clarity, control, trust, and a sense of support                                                                                |

The brand must not become an authoritarian expert or an emotionally dependent
assistant.

## Brand Personality

### Core characteristics

- calm;
- reliable;
- precise;
- modern;
- intelligent;
- human;
- respectful;
- unobtrusive.

### Excluded characteristics

- coldness;
- preachiness;
- anxiety;
- infantility;
- excessive technicity;
- aggressive advertising.

## Brand Positioning

Diabetes Universe is a calm, modern, and intelligent digital companion that users
trust every day.

The brand is **not** positioned as:

- a medical clinic;
- a hospital;
- a pharmaceutical company;
- another generic AI startup;
- a children's application;
- a fitness application.

## Visual Philosophy

The visual system must be perceived as:

- modern;
- calm;
- precise;
- minimalist;
- technological without futuristic noise;
- human without childishness;
- medically responsible without clinical clichés.

Forms must be:

- simple;
- cohesive;
- confident;
- scalable;
- easily recognizable;
- suitable for motion and animation.

The brand must also be recognizable through form, composition, rhythm, space,
motion, iconography, illustration, typography, and interaction character—not
decoration alone.

This section consolidates approved principles only. It does not define final
colors, typefaces, logos, or UI components.

## Logo Architecture Summary

See [06 Logo Architecture Specification](06-logo-architecture-specification.md).

Approved logo architecture principles:

- the symbol must be recognizable without text;
- mandatory requirements include 16×16, favicon, app icon, monochrome, and
  scale from favicon to outdoor advertising use;
- exactly three concept directions are approved: **Universe**, **Orbit**, and
  **DU Symbol**;
- concept development and evaluation follow mandatory sequential workflows;
- no winning concept is selected in the current approved specifications;
- no final logo, SVG, or app icon is produced in this Brand Book.

## Brand Identity Summary

See [07 Brand Identity Specification](07-brand-identity-specification.md).

Approved identity system principles:

- **Color strategy roles** — deep blue, indigo, teal accent, graphite, warm white
  (no HEX/RGB values defined here);
- **Typography strategy** — modern grotesque, high readability, digital
  optimization, international support, minimalism (no typeface family selected
  here);
- **Asset hierarchy** — Brand Symbol, Wordmark, Combined Mark, Iconography,
  Illustration, Motion, Photography;
- iconography, illustration, photography, and motion follow minimalist,
  informative, accessible principles defined in document `07`;
- digital and print usage must preserve hierarchy, contrast, and scalability.

This summary does not define concrete colors, fonts, icons, illustrations, or
photographs.

## Brand Governance Summary

See [08 Brand Governance Specification](08-brand-governance-specification.md).

Approved brand governance rules:

- **ChatGPT** owns brand architecture integrity; **Cursor** implements approved
  repository changes within scope;
- changes to documents `05`–`07` require mandatory architectural approval and
  the full approved lifecycle;
- brand specifications use semantic versioning: **v1.0**, **v1.x**, **v2.0**;
- brand changes become effective only after **Feature Complete**;
- assets, usage compliance, exceptions, deprecation, documentation updates, and
  periodic audits are governed by document `08`.

## Brand Usage Principles

All brand application must:

- comply with documents `05`–`08` and this Brand Book;
- use the approved asset hierarchy without unapproved variants;
- preserve immutable brand decisions and international neutrality;
- apply the same tone, writing, accessibility, and internationalization rules
  across surfaces;
- avoid medical clichés, visual noise, and independent sub-brands;
- request documented, time-bounded exceptions when deviation is unavoidable;
- not publish materials that fail usage compliance or audit requirements.

This Brand Book does not create assets. It defines how approved rules apply.

## Tone of Voice Summary

Tone of voice must be:

- calm;
- clear;
- respectful;
- precise;
- supportive.

Communication must also be concise, accurate, and neutral to age, gender, country,
and diabetes type.

### Prohibited tone

- dramatization;
- aggressive marketing;
- medical promises;
- excessive emotionality;
- intimidation;
- blaming the user;
- categorical diagnoses;
- treatment prescriptions;
- artificial emotionality;
- excessive AI terminology;
- advertising noise.

Writing must use short sentences, plain language, and consistent terminology.

## Accessibility Summary

Brand accessibility is mandatory.

The brand must support:

- WCAG-aligned contrast and readability;
- sufficient contrast between text, icons, and backgrounds;
- readable and scalable type;
- clear perception for users with visual impairments;
- recognition at small sizes and in monochrome modes;
- understanding of medical information without aesthetic compromise.

Aesthetics never take priority over safety, readability, or accessibility.

## Internationalization Summary

The brand must work equally well:

- in multiple languages and writing systems;
- in LTR and potentially RTL interfaces;
- across different cultural contexts;
- without dependence on English wordplay;
- without symbolism understandable in only one country.

Brand communication and visual decisions must remain culturally neutral.

## Brand Do & Don't

### Do

- use the official name **Diabetes Universe**;
- follow documents `05`–`08` and this Brand Book;
- communicate with calm, clear, respectful, precise, supportive tone;
- preserve trust, accuracy, simplicity, reliability, and care;
- design for international, long-term, multi-platform use;
- separate information from medical recommendation;
- show uncertainty and AI limitations where they exist;
- support accessibility and WCAG-aligned contrast;
- use approved logo concept directions and identity principles only;
- document and time-bound any approved exception.

### Don't

- rebrand or use alternative names;
- position the brand as a clinic, hospital, pharma company, generic AI startup,
  children's app, or fitness app;
- use medical clichés (blood drop, syringe, cross, heart, pill, stethoscope, etc.);
- use clip art, random geometry, decorative gradients, or short-term trends;
- use a direct D/U letter monogram for the logo symbol;
- use medical green as the primary brand color;
- make medical promises, diagnoses, or treatment prescriptions;
- dramatize, intimidate, blame, or create guilt;
- introduce visual noise, inconsistent styles, or unapproved sub-brands;
- publish non-compliant materials or silent exceptions;
- create final logo, color, font, icon, illustration, or photo assets in this
  document.

## Success Criteria

The Brand Book is successful when:

- it serves as the official consolidated brand documentation without creating new
  architectural decisions;
- brand overview, strategy, DNA, personality, and positioning match documents
  `05`–`08` without contradiction;
- logo, identity, and governance summaries link to authoritative specifications;
- usage principles, tone, accessibility, internationalization, and Do & Don't
  rules are explicit;
- version history records **Brand Book v1.0**;
- documentation navigation reflects the Brand Book entry;
- no final graphic assets, colors, fonts, icons, illustrations, or document `10`
  work are produced in this stage;
- contradictions with approved architecture are reported as **Blocked** instead
  of being resolved through independent brand changes.
